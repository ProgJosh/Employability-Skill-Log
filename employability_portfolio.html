About this portfolio

This portfolio highlights the employability skills I practised and developed during the module. Through research, creative work and collaboration, I gained a better understanding of how transferable skills contribute to completing tasks and communicating ideas effectively. It also records areas I want to strengthen as I take on future projects.

Skill 01

Communication

I practised communicating ideas clearly while developing the message and content of our video. I considered how to make information about responsible online behaviour understandable to an audience.

The video moves from an everyday filming scenario to a short checklist. That sequence gave viewers a practical way to apply ideas about privacy, music and disclosure before they post.

Skill 02

Digital Literacy

I used digital tools to support the creation and presentation of our video project. Working with editing software and AI support helped me understand where each tool fits in a production workflow.

Willingness to learn came through learning how a layered editing timeline brings together video, titles, narration and music.

Skill 03

Research and Information Literacy

I explored online privacy, social media responsibility and the effects of sharing content. The project drew on Philippine laws and advertising standards, a legal commentary, and international sources on endorsements and AI transparency.

Checking the scope of each reference helped me practise persistence and make the information I shared more trustworthy.

Research sources

  1. Philippine laws on social media influencer marketing, endorsements and consumer protection
  2. FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255)
  3. Ad Standards Council Code of Ethics
  4. Consumer Act of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 7394)
  5. Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)
  6. EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)

Skill 04

Teamwork and Collaboration

I worked alongside other group members towards a shared outcome. I contributed to researching the topic, developing content and supporting the video’s production.

Listening to different ideas and coordinating our work reinforced the importance of following instructions and being dependable within a group.

Skill 05

Creativity and Problem-Solving

I helped communicate a broad topic in a short video format. The street-food scenario gave viewers a familiar setting, and the final checklist reduced the main points to four decisions they could remember.

Thinking about the order and presentation of ideas helped me see how creative choices can make a message easier to use.

Skill 06

Organisation and Time Management

I practised tracking work against module deadlines. The submission record shows that I submitted Task 1 on 5 October 2026, one day and 20 hours before the deadline on 7 October.

Giving myself time before the deadline helped me practise prioritisation, meet a commitment and take responsibility for my work.

Task 2 · Group documentary

Before You Hit Post

A short documentary about the choices people make when they film, edit and share content. It uses a street-food stall to explore privacy, music licensing, advertising disclosure and the environmental impact of streaming.

Reflective summary

Reflecting on my growth

Gibbs’ Reflective Cycle · 155 words

Using Gibbs’ reflective cycle, I reflected on my experience completing the “Before You Hit Post” video project. The project allowed me to develop skills in research, communication, digital literacy, teamwork and organisation. I contributed to researching the topic, developing content and supporting the video’s production.

I found that working collaboratively helped me consider different ideas and understand how individual contributions support a shared outcome. However, I also recognised that managing tasks and communicating clearly throughout a project are important, particularly when coordinating work and meeting deadlines.

I learned to use digital tools more purposefully and to think critically about how information can be presented to an audience. This experience strengthened my confidence in contributing to creative tasks and made me more aware of the employability skills I use and those I still need to improve. In future projects, I will plan my tasks earlier, communicate more proactively and seek feedback to continue developing these skills.
